About

Behind every reliable product is a strong QA process.

I'm a Manual QA Engineer with 7+ years of experience protecting the quality of web, mobile, banking and payment systems — from core platform modules to POS terminals and end-to-end payment flows.

My work sits at the intersection of business requirements, user expectations, and technical reality. I test with a clear structure — test plans, test cases, traceable execution — but I also explore, question, and look for the edge cases that slip through specifications.

I've validated core banking workflows (loans, accounts, transaction history, user management, reporting), mobile banking apps on iOS and Android, POS terminals with EMV cards across BE, NL, LUX and CZ markets, and eCommerce transactions from both issuer and acquirer perspectives.

What I bring to a team is careful attention to detail, structured problem-solving, and clear communication of defects and risks — so developers, team leads and stakeholders can move forward with confidence.

Selected Case Studies

How I approach quality.

Representative case studies based on my professional experience. They illustrate scope, approach and the kinds of risks I look for — without disclosing confidential client details.

Case Study 01

Core Banking Quality Assurance

Domain Web-based core banking platform
Scope Loans, accounts, transaction history, user management, reporting
Approach Functional validation, UI testing, regression runs, data consistency checks against backend modules
Risk areas Incorrect balances, broken filters, reporting mismatches, permission gaps in user management

Structured QA protects data integrity and prevents costly downstream errors in financial workflows.

Case Study 02

POS & Payment Transaction Testing

Domain POS terminals · EMV · Payment protocols
Scope EMV transactions, terminal configuration, protocol validation, end-to-end verification
Approach Testing with CTEP, VIC and TLV simulators across BE, NL, LUX and CZ markets
Risk areas Protocol mismatches, incorrect terminal configuration, transaction validation failures

Payment workflows leave no room for ambiguity — careful validation protects both the product and its users.

Case Study 03

Mobile Banking Application Testing

Domain iOS & Android banking apps
Scope Usability, functional behavior, filters, date pickers, data consistency
Approach Manual testing on both platforms, cross-checking app data against backend modules and web counterparts
Risk areas Platform-specific UI issues, filter inconsistencies, date handling errors, sync gaps with backend

Mobile QA is as much about consistency between surfaces as it is about individual screen behavior.
